How to Play

  • Click a tile to select it, then click an adjacent tile to move
  • Capture territory by moving your army into enemy tiles
  • Your General and Cities generate troops every turn
  • All your land generates +1 troop every 25 turns
  • Capture the enemy General to win!

Desktop: Click to select/move, WASD/arrows to queue moves, Q to cancel. Right-click or Shift+drag to pan, scroll to zoom.

Mobile: Tap to select, drag to queue moves. Pinch to zoom, two-finger drag to pan.
